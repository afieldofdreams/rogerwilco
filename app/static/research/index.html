The evidence behind rogerwilco, including the part that went against it

Four studies, run in sequence over five days, each answering the question the previous one raised. Every corpus is synthetic, every harness and log is in the repository, and the third study includes the control that overturned the first write-up's headline.

1. Wire study

Does a closed-vocabulary line beat English, JSON, glyphs or a positional form on tokens, round trip and decision parity?

240synthetic handoffs, five formats, two models
29% to 60%fewer tokens than English for the same facts
240/240round trip exact; parity 100% on Sonnet

Keyed form won over positional because a small model miscounted positional columns in one run of two; three-letter codes won over glyphs because glyph savings were tokenizer-dependent and the legend cost more.

2. Readback pilot

Does a comprehension readback catch a misread brief before the work is done, and at what cost in tokens and false alarms?

36trials with planted faults, blind grading
4/4handoff misreads caught by readback, typed or prose
0false alarms from the typed readback on Sonnet

Readback verifies understanding, not execution: an agent stated the constraint it then failed. Comprehension readback costs about twice the tokens of a bare handoff with the legend in the prompt, and five times with the full skill file.

3. Month-end close

When four agents prepare a payment run under a policy that reserves decisions for a human, where does the saving actually come from?

28runs, eight regimes, two scenarios, two models
33%of free-prose traffic under the seven rules, in plain English
22k to 1.9ktokens of files per run once the lines are the record

The saving is the discipline, not the notation: disciplined prose matched the lines on the human's reading and beat them on the agents' traffic and compute. The notation buys verification, which the strong model did not need and the weak model did, and even then not enough. It also buys translation: when the extractor turns each email into one line with a source pointer and no agent writes a working file, the files the agents pass around fall from 22k tokens a run to under 2k with the same outcome. Includes an adversarial scenario with an injected instruction and a wrong answer from the principal.

4. Translation

Can a clinical document become one line per statement, with its attributes, values and the exact words behind it, completely, accurately and the same every time?

144translations of twelve documents, 422 gold assertions, lines and JSON, Sonnet and Haiku
99.7%of assertions found by Sonnet, 99% citing the exact words, nothing invented
70 of 72Haiku first attempts refused by the gate; made whole in form, still wrong in meaning

With a strong model the lines are the record: complete, traceable to the words, identical across runs for 89% of assertions. Lines and JSON came out the same and cost the same per assertion. The record is 5.6 times the size of the note, so what the lines buy is structure and provenance, not compression.
