A Crox lab

rogerwilco is made by Crox, an AI research lab in London. We build AI for work where getting it wrong costs real money or real care, and we publish what we find, including the parts that go against us.

Why this

Crox is run by Adam Field, a former commercial airline pilot who spent a decade shipping products in regulated sectors: healthcare, legal, fintech, insurance. Aviation handles handovers between people with a readback and a cross-check, because the cost of a misheard clearance is not recoverable. When AI agents started handing work to each other in paragraphs, that looked like the same problem without the same discipline. rogerwilco is the discipline, written down so a program can enforce it, and tested.

The format has a lineage. M-Form is a semantic compression format for medical records; P-Form applied the idea to handovers inside a knowledge graph; rogerwilco is the general version, with a readback. The six studies on the research page are the evidence, and the first write-up's headline was overturned by its own control. That is how we would rather find out.

How we work

Small studies with real controls, synthetic data, every log and grade in the repository. Everything in rogerwilco is open: the spec, the skill, the codec, the research.
