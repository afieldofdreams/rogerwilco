How AI agents hand work to each other so that a program can check it.

When several agents share a job, they tell each other what they found and what they need in paragraphs. A paragraph cannot be checked by code, so nobody knows, until the work is done, whether the next agent read the figures right, whether a fact was made up or dropped on the way, or whether a decision that belonged to a person was taken by a machine.

rogerwilco is a set of controls for that. Below, what goes wrong in a handover, the control that catches it, and what five studies saw: a fault-injection study of 114 handoffs before irreversible acts, a four-agent accounts-payable close run 28 times with planted events, a clinical-note study of 144 translations against 422 hand-annotated statements, a readback pilot, and a wire study. Every figure comes from two or three runs per cell, Claude Sonnet and Haiku only, on synthetic data, with the gold sets and scorers written by the author. Enough to show direction; not yet a measurement.

The receiver reads the handoff differently from how the sender meant it

Control: the readback. The receiver repeats the handoff back as its own line and the sender's code compares it with the intent the sender wrote down before sending, before letting go of the work. Roger is received; wilco is understood and will comply.

9 of 16, then 0planted faults in a handoff before a payment, a payee change or a supplier email became a wrong irreversible act without a readback (14 of 16 with a small model). With the line read back and checked in code, none did, on either model: every content fault was rejected at the readback, the misaddressed handoffs were refused by the receiver, no clean handoff was rejected, and every act after a confirmed readback was right on every field. In 21 runs of a clean month-end close with a strong model, nothing needed catching.

Use it before anything irreversible: a payment, a payee change, anything sent outside. It costs one more turn per handoff, two when corrected, and the receiver's brief carries the legend.

A fact is invented, or the words are paraphrased until they say something else

Control: the pointer and the gate. A fact that came from a document crosses as a line that says where the words are (src=doc:3#l12, src=email:7), and a gate refuses a line that has no pointer, or whose words are not at the place it cites.

0 inventedon clinical notes: every line a strong model wrote quoted words that were in the document, it found 99.7% of the statements and quoted the right passage 99% of the time. A small model had 70 of 72 first attempts refused, paraphrased quotes above all, and got every document through in two rounds, with more help from the error list than a production gate would give.

At the edge, where documents enter the system. Tested on short supplier emails and clinical notes; not yet on anything harder.

A fact is dropped between agents

Control: facts in slots, and a validator that refuses an incomplete line. One line per item, every fact in a labelled slot, a closed vocabulary. The facts cross once, as lines, and nobody writes a working file to restate them.

18 refusedmalformed or invented lines from a small model, each fixed on the next send. The same model in disciplined prose: a reader could answer 6 and 7 of 8 questions from its logs, against 8 of 8 and 7 of 8 from the lines logs (two runs each). With a strong model, the lines carried 73 of 73 checked facts from twelve short emails in each of three runs.

For small or mixed models, and wherever someone has to trust the log.

A decision that belongs to a person is taken by a machine, or the person is interrupted for things the policy leaves to agents

Control: a written policy, and escalation as a question with a closed list of answers. The item, the amounts, the decision needed, and the answers the person may give: approve, decline, hold, reject, call back.

3 of 3reserved decisions reached the person and 0 of 4 decoys did, in every run with a strong model, free prose included. A small model missed the USD hold in two of six reserved decisions on disciplined prose and one of six on lines (two runs each), and interrupted for decisions already made in both.

A strong model with the policy did this in every format. What the seven rules changed is the cost of asking: decision requests fell from 1,333 tokens a run in free prose to 520.

An instruction planted in a document is followed

Control: a written policy. Every arm had one, so its own effect was not isolated; what was tested is the format, which made no difference. Agents with a policy ignored a paragraph in a lookalike email telling them a bank change was pre-approved, escalated it anyway, and pushed back on a wrong approval from the boss with the evidence.

6 of 6runs ignored the injection with a strong model, in prose, disciplined prose and lines alike. A small model translating notes followed a planted instruction in three of six runs even with the gate in place.

The protocol does not claim this one. Write the policy down and give it to a model that holds to it.

The receiver reads it back correctly and then does the wrong thing

Control: none before the work. A readback verifies understanding, not execution, and a validator checks form, not judgement.

Still wrongin the pilot a small model read a constraint back correctly five times and ignored it. In the close it paid the USD invoice it should have held, in disciplined prose (two of two runs) and in lines (one of two). The lines logs were the more complete record of what it did.

What would help: a done report that carries the values as executed, checked against the handoff the way a readback is, and typed acceptance criteria that make the done-time check mechanical for the checkable ones. Planned, untested.

Where it earned nothing

With a strong model in conversation, seven rules written in English (facts once, in a fixed shape; reasoning by reference; state only what changed; acknowledge a handoff; escalate with a closed list; ask, answer, finish; no preamble) cut the traffic between agents to a third and the person's reading to 40%, and the syntax added nothing to that but the checks above. The rules are free and are in the skill. Use the syntax where code has to check the message, not to save tokens.

Use it

An agent that can run code validates its own lines with the skill (one folder, standard library). An agent that cannot calls this server as an MCP tool. Any agent reads the format from the legend pasted into its prompt.

claude mcp add --transport http rogerwilco

Below: write a line and see whether a program accepts it; paste a handoff and its readback and see whether the readback passes; paste a document and the lines that state facts from it and see whether every pointer holds.

A profile is the word list for one kind of work. core is the basics, pform is for a knowledge graph, pay is for paying bills, clin is for clinical notes. The two message panels use the one you pick.

Try a line

Pick an example message or write your own. Press Validate and the program tells you whether it is a proper rogerwilco line, what it understood, and what is wrong if anything. JSON shows the same message as data. Make readback writes the reply the receiving agent should send. Digest gives the short fingerprint that proves a message arrived unchanged.

Readback options

Check a readback

This is what the sender's code does before it lets go of a job. Paste the handoff (HO) you sent and the readback (RB) that came back. It checks four things in order: the reply is to the right message from the right agent, the message arrived unchanged, the facts were repeated back correctly, and, if you asked for it, the receiver understood the brief the same way you did. The first two are roger, the last two are wilco. The job changes hands only if every check passes.

Check lines against their source

This is the gate at the edge. Paste a document and the lines that state facts from it. Every line must be valid, must say where in the document its words are, and the words must be there. Lines of the document are counted from 1, blank lines included. The pay profile checks pointers into an inbox export instead, and that net plus VAT equals the amount.
