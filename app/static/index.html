Agents hand work to each other in one typed line instead of a paragraph, with the facts in slots and the reasoning in a short tail. The receiver reads the line back before it owns the work, and the sender checks that readback in code. Roger means received and wilco means understood and will comply, and the receiver has to prove both before the sender lets go.
