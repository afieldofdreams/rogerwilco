# We get AI features through compliance and into production

It is Monday morning, the demo went well, and your AI feature is still stuck in a compliance review that keeps sending it back. Nobody can say what would get it through, and the launch date has moved again. At Crox, we have shipped AI in regulated industries, including health and financial services, so we know what reviewers need to see before they say yes. We start from the review, not from the model. We agree the evidence with your compliance, legal and security teams first, then build the feature to produce it. We test on real cases, not demo cases, set clear limits on what the system may do, and monitor its behaviour after launch, so reliability is something you can show rather than hope for. Then we hand over something your own team can run and your reviewers can trust. That is how a demo becomes a rollout.
