# We get AI features through compliance review and into production

On Monday morning, the AI feature that impressed everyone in the demo is still stuck, because the compliance review keeps sending it back. We know how this goes. The gap between a working demo and a reliable rollout is rarely the model. It is the evidence reviewers ask for, the failure cases nobody tested, and the monitoring nobody owns. At Crox, we have shipped AI in regulated industries, including health and financial services, so we plan for review from week one. We work alongside your team to define what the feature must never do, prove it with tests and records, and watch its behaviour after launch. Then your reviewers get answers and your customers get a feature that keeps working.
