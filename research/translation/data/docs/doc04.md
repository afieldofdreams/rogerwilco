I saw Ms Barrington today with four months of epigastric discomfort after meals and
bloating. She has no dysphagia, no weight loss and no melaena, and she has not vomited. She
has been taking ibuprofen over the counter two or three times a week for headaches. She
drinks about 8 units of alcohol a week and does not smoke. There is no family history of
gastric cancer.

Examination was unremarkable: abdomen soft, mild epigastric tenderness, no masses.

The stool antigen test you arranged is positive for H. pylori. Her haemoglobin is 131 g/L
and ferritin 44.

I have prescribed eradication therapy for seven days: omeprazole 20 mg once daily,
amoxicillin 1 g twice daily and clarithromycin 500 mg twice daily. I have advised her to
stop the ibuprofen and to use paracetamol for her headaches instead. Given her age and the
absence of alarm features I have not arranged an endoscopy.

Please arrange a repeat stool antigen test no sooner than four weeks after she finishes the
course, and at least two weeks off any acid suppression. If it remains positive, please
refer her back and we will use second-line therapy.

I have not arranged routine follow-up in clinic.
