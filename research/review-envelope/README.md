# Study 11: the review envelope

Status, 27 November 2026: design in progress. No protocol is registered yet and no registered trial has run.

What is here so far:

- `tools/` holds the case generator (`casefile.py`), the plan builder, the runner and the grader, written while the design was being settled. They may change before registration.
- `calibration/` holds exploratory runs made to find out where an effect could be seen at all. They were run before any hypothesis was fixed, on payments that are held out of the pilot and the main run, and they will not be pooled with either.

The word envelope here is the aviation one: the limits inside which an act goes ahead with no reviewer, and outside which something or someone must look. It is not the message envelope of `SPEC.md` section 5, which is the addressing slots of a line.

The question, in one sentence: when a reviewer stands in front of a payment that cannot be recalled, what does it stop, as a function of what it is shown, and what does that say about which acts need a reviewer at all.

The protocol, with its hypotheses and tests, will replace this note in a commit of its own before the pilot.
