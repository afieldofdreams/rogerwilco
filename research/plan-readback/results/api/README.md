# The cross-vendor run: gpt-5.5 and gpt-5.4-mini through the API harness, partial

The registered design built afresh for two OpenAI receivers and run with `tools/run_api.py` over `research/harness`, Claude Sonnet 5.5 as the judge through `claude -p`. 2,196 of the 2,762 trials ran: blocks 1 and 2 near complete (block 2 cells hold 160 to 191 of their 215 trials), block 3 not started. The key's credits ran out twice, at trial 1,230 on 11 October 2026 and again after a top-up; the trials that failed on credits were reset to their built state and the 566 that never ran stay built, so the run resumes from where it stopped if it is ever finished. `summary.json` and `summary.md` are `tools/analyse.py --tag api --only=-gpt --exclude=-x` over the graded trials.

What is in the run as graded:

- Every trial ran in a single process per credit top-up, six at a time. 210 trials ended held (a readback rejected twice, no act), 182 of them gpt-5.4-mini in arm D; 5 ended with no second readback file after the receiver said READBACK; 1 ended with the receiver describing what it was about to do instead of a reply word. 182 gpt-5.4-mini trials ended their turn through the harness's done tool without a reply word, and the runner inferred the word from the files written (`inferred_reply` in `turns.json`).
- Tokens through the API: gpt-5.5 40.3 million prompt and 1.09 million completion tokens over 1,082 trials; gpt-5.4-mini 40.2 million prompt and 0.61 million completion over 1,114. The harness records no cost for these models; the prompt tokens are the harness re-reading the trial's context on every call, not the protocol's traffic.
- The sender's codec was frozen during the run. The resumed process imported the protocol's live codec after the `exec` readback level was merged that morning, and its tier 5 held 24 gpt-5.5 clean bank-change trials in arm D on readbacks that carried every value, demanding `prev` where the study's receivers carry `x_prev`. Those 24 were reset and are among the 566 unrun. `tools/frozen/` now holds the codec and profiles at the frozen commit and the sender checks with them. The receiver's own `make-rb` and `validate` ran against the live codec; `receiver_codec_after_change.txt` lists the 169 arm-D trials whose receiver ran after the change (73 gpt-5.5, 96 gpt-5.4-mini). For gpt-5.4-mini it changes nothing in the outcome, since its execution readbacks never carried the resolved slots in either period; for gpt-5.5 the changed `make-rb` pre-filled the handover's own slots, which it was copying correctly anyway.
- Grader validation was not repeated on these receivers; the graders are the frozen ones, validated on the Claude main run.
